# Thai retail iPhone part-number checks — 2026-11-21

The owner requires Thai retail devices only, with an M prefix. N, numeric prefixes and other prefixes are not accepted. This is BESTCHOICE's intake policy; it is not a claim that every M device is new, unmodified or originally sold in Thailand.

At the owner’s request, customers do not enter a part number. The collapsed help explains the M prefix and generation suffix; customers confirm Thai retail origin and the M-prefix policy by selecting the Thai option. Public quote/submit validate that origin answer, not an uncollected code. ZP/A alone does not prove the country of sale: regional codes can be shared. Customers who are unsure can ask staff for help through LINE.

Apple's own instructions distinguish the part number shown in Settings > General > About from the A-number toggled by tapping the row: https://support.apple.com/th-th/106343 . Do not reject an A-number as a device type; tell the customer how to switch back.

## Enforcement

- No part-number input, payload field or part-number snapshot is required or collected by the form.
- Public quote and submit validate server-owned origin choices. Missing origin configuration fails closed.
- Imported or unsure answers cannot continue to a quote or submit.
- The M-prefix rule is stated in the banner, help and Thai confirmation. Staff verify the physical device; the form cannot automatically verify a code that it does not collect.
- Internal staff calculations keep compatibility with historical assessments.
- The policy does not replace in-store inspection or proof of Thai retail origin.
